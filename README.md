<h1 align="center" style="font-family:Arial, sans-serif;">🧠 Quiz Trivia</h1>

<p align="center" style="font-family:Arial, sans-serif; font-size:16px;">
Quiz Trivia is a fun and engaging Android app that lets you test your knowledge across various topics. Challenge yourself, compete with friends, and track your progress! Built with <b>Kotlin</b> and <b>Jetpack Compose</b>.
</p>

---

<h2 style="font-family:Arial, sans-serif;">✨ Features</h2>

<ul style="font-family:Arial, sans-serif; font-size:14px;">
<li>🎯 <b>Multiple Categories:</b> Science, History, Movies, Sports, and more!</li>
<li>⏱️ <b>Timed Quizzes:</b> Test your speed and accuracy under time limits.</li>
<li>🏆 <b>Score Tracking:</b> Keep track of your high scores and progress.</li>
<li>💡 <b>Instant Feedback:</b> See correct answers immediately and learn as you play.</li>
<li>🖤 <b>Clean UI:</b> Simple, modern interface powered by Jetpack Compose.</li>
<li>📱 <b>Offline Mode:</b> Play anywhere without an internet connection.</li>
</ul>

---


<h2 style="font-family:Arial, sans-serif;">🚀 How to Use</h2>

<ol style="font-family:Arial, sans-serif; font-size:14px;">
<li>Clone the repo: <code>git clone https://github.com/alionureskiocak/quiz-trivia.git</code></li>
<li>Open the project in <b>Android Studio</b>.</li>
<li>Build and run the app on an emulator or real device.</li>
<li>Select a category and start the quiz!</li>
<li>Answer questions before the timer runs out and track your score.</li>
</ol>
